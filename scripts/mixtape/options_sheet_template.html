Mixtape: choose the deck

Four decisions, in order. Deck style: which era the cassette deck comes from. Door: whether a smoked cassette door sits over the tape well or the tape is bare. Extras: which panel details earn their space (a tape counter, transport keys, VU meters). Tape scale: how wide the cassette is as a share of the screen, since a big tape and visible extras compete for the frame. Section E shows how different tapes read in each deck, and Section G collects your picks into text you can paste back to Claude.

These are Eevee previews, not final Cycles quality: expect softer shadows and simpler reflections in the finished renders. The label strip is a known placeholder: its printed block is narrower than the label and will be re-authored, so please ignore it when judging.
